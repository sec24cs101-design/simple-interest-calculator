# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in our project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
Expected behavior includes respectful communication, constructive feedback, collaboration, and consideration for others.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and sharing private information without permission.

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and responding to reports of unacceptable behavior.

## Scope
This code of conduct applies to project spaces and public interactions related to the project.

## Enforcement
Reports may be directed to the project maintainers. Reports will be reviewed fairly and handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement Guidelines
Depending on the seriousness of a violation, maintainers may issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or remove contributions.
