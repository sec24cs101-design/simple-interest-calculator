# Contributing Guidelines

Contributions to this project are welcome. All contributions, bug reports, bug fixes, documentation improvements, enhancements, and ideas are appreciated.

## How to Contribute

1. Fork the repository.
2. Clone your fork to your local machine.
3. Create a new branch for your changes.
4. Make your changes and test them.
5. Commit your changes with a clear and meaningful message.
6. Push your branch to GitHub.
7. Open a pull request describing your changes.

## Bug Reports

When reporting a bug, explain the problem, provide steps to reproduce it, and describe the expected and actual results.

## Bug Fixes and Enhancements

Explain the issue or proposed improvement and test your changes before submitting them.

## Documentation Improvements

Improvements to clarity, spelling, examples, and project instructions are welcome.

## Ideas and Feedback

Share ideas that could improve the project, including the reason for the proposed change.

## Review Process

Contributions may be reviewed for correctness, clarity, and consistency before they are merged.
